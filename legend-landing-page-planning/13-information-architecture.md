# 13 — Information Architecture

## Phase-one route map
The launch can remain a single route:

```text
/
```

Everything important is reachable through anchors.

## Recommended anchor IDs
```text
#top
#featured
#editions
#stories
#developers-diary
#about
#verticals
#pitch
#partnerships
#contact
```

## Header behavior
Desktop:
- logo left;
- center/right nav;
- CTA right.

Mobile:
- logo;
- menu button;
- drawer with section links and CTA.

## Potential future routes
Do not build these now unless requested:
```text
/issues/[slug]
/stories/[slug]
/people/[slug]
/categories/[slug]
```

The local content model should be clean enough that these routes could be added later without rewriting the data.

## Page flow logic

1. **Hero** — identify brand.
2. **Featured people** — prove credibility.
3. **Latest editions** — show the publication.
4. **Featured stories** — show editorial depth.
5. **Developers' Diary** — reinforce a recognizable series.
6. **Why LEGEND** — explain editorial point of view.
7. **Verticals** — show breadth.
8. **Pitch Your Story** — editorial conversion.
9. **Partnerships** — commercial conversion.
10. **Footer** — contact and trust.

## Why forms come late
Putting “Get Featured” at the top would make the publication feel transactional.
The website should establish editorial legitimacy before asking for submissions.
