# 10 — Content Architecture

## Recommended editorial verticals

### 1. Leadership
Public administrators, institutional leaders and decision-makers.

### 2. Business & Finance
Founders, investors, banking and enterprise.

### 3. Developers' Diary
Real-estate developers, urban builders and built-environment leadership.

### 4. Enterprise & Legacy
Business families, legacy companies and generational transition.

### 5. Mumbai & Infrastructure
Housing, transport, city-building and development institutions.

### 6. Culture & Public Life
Public personalities, creators and cultural figures.

### 7. Design & Architecture
Designers, architects, spaces and aesthetics.

### 8. Social Impact
Foundations, communities and purpose-led initiatives.

### 9. Lifestyle & Hospitality
Restaurants, hospitality and urban lifestyle.

### 10. Sport & Community
Sport, clubs, community concepts and new urban experiences.

## Homepage content hierarchy
The landing page does not need to expose every category equally.

Prioritize:
1. latest edition;
2. featured people;
3. lead stories;
4. Developers' Diary;
5. editorial philosophy;
6. broader verticals.

## Content object hierarchy
Each story should locally store:
- issue;
- category;
- title;
- subject/person;
- role/organisation where relevant;
- short excerpt;
- image;
- optional link or PDF page;
- featured flag.

## Homepage wording
Use:
- Featured in LEGEND
- Latest Edition
- From the Pages of LEGEND
- Developers' Diary
- Stories That Matter
- Explore the Edition

Avoid:
- Latest news
- Breaking
- Trending gossip
unless the brand explicitly shifts in that direction later.
