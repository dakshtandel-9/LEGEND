# Source Notes

The planning pack was derived from the two supplied LEGEND issues.

## Source files
1. `Legend_May_Edition_01_compressed(3).pdf`
2. `Legend_July_Edition_01(3).pdf`

## Source-supported brand facts used
- LEGEND is presented as “The Magazine of Extraordinary Stories.”
- The issues use “Excellence · Culture · Vision.”
- May 2026 is Issue 01.
- July 2026 is Issue 02.
- The publication is presented as monthly.
- Mumbai is the publication location/context.
- The supplied issues contain a recurring “Developers' Diary” feature.
- The editorial mix includes public leadership, business, finance, real estate/developers, culture/public life, social impact, design, hospitality/lifestyle and sport/community.

## Important interpretation
The digital color values, design system, website copy, information architecture and technical architecture in this pack are recommendations created for the website. They are not claimed to be official LEGEND brand guidelines.

## Important editorial distinction
“Developers' Diary” in these issues refers to property / urban-development figures, not software developers.
