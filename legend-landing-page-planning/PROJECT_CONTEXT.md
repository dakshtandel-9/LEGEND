# LEGEND Landing Page — Project Context

## Project type
A premium, editorial-style **single landing page** for LEGEND magazine, Mumbai.

## Non-negotiable technical rules
- Landing page only.
- No CRM.
- No database.
- No Supabase/Firebase.
- No admin panel.
- No authentication.
- No custom backend.
- No API routes unless a future requirement explicitly demands one.
- Forms use **Google Forms** only.
- Images are stored locally in the project under `/public/images`.
- Magazine PDFs can be stored locally under `/public/issues`.
- Deploy on Vercel.
- Prefer a statically renderable Next.js page.
- Content should live in local TypeScript/JSON data files so it is easy to update without a CMS.

## Brand direction
LEGEND should feel like a modern digital translation of a premium print magazine:
- editorial, not SaaS;
- refined, not flashy;
- people and photography first;
- strong serif display typography;
- restrained motion;
- ivory, black and warm gold master palette;
- edition-specific accent colours may appear in issue cards and story details.

## Source material
Planning is based on:
- `Legend_May_Edition_01_compressed(3).pdf`
- `Legend_July_Edition_01(3).pdf`

The source magazines position LEGEND around:
- extraordinary stories;
- excellence, culture and vision;
- leadership;
- enterprise;
- Mumbai and urban development;
- real estate / Developers' Diary;
- finance;
- culture and public life;
- design;
- lifestyle;
- social impact.

## Primary website goals
1. Establish LEGEND as a credible premium publication.
2. Show recognizable people and stories already featured.
3. Present current and previous editions.
4. Make the magazine's editorial positioning clear.
5. Let people pitch a story through Google Forms.
6. Let brands enquire about advertising/partnerships through a separate Google Form.
7. Give the publication a polished link it can share with public figures, founders, brands and readers.

## Editorial credibility rule
Use wording such as **“Featured in LEGEND”** unless an item is confirmed to be an exclusive interview.
Never imply that every person shown was interviewed directly.

## Implementation priority
The website should look excellent on mobile because links are likely to be shared through Instagram, WhatsApp and other social platforms.
