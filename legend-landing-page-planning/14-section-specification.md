# 14 — Section Specification

## 01. Header

### Purpose
Navigation + strong masthead presence.

### Content
- LEGEND logo;
- Stories;
- Editions;
- Developers' Diary;
- About;
- Partner With Us;
- Pitch Your Story CTA.

### Behavior
- transparent/ivory at top;
- becomes compact sticky header after scroll;
- 1px divider on sticky state.

---

## 02. Hero

### Layout
Desktop:
- 5-column copy;
- 7-column visual;
- large negative space;
- issue covers layered near the main image.

Mobile:
- copy first;
- visual second;
- CTA stack or two-column if space allows.

### Visual
One approved hero portrait / editorial composition.
Do not use ten small faces.

### Copy
Use the hero copy from `12-copy-deck.md`.

---

## 03. Featured in LEGEND

### Layout
Option A:
Horizontal portrait rail.

Option B:
Editorial grid with one large profile and smaller supporting profiles.

Recommended:
One large + four compact on desktop; horizontal scroll on mobile.

### Card fields
- portrait;
- name;
- short category;
- issue month/year.

---

## 04. Latest Editions

### Layout
Two large issue cards for launch.

Each:
- cover;
- issue number;
- month/year;
- short summary;
- 2–3 names;
- issue accent;
- View Edition CTA.

### PDF behavior
Open local PDF in a new tab.
Do not load full PDFs on initial page render.

---

## 05. Stories That Matter

### Layout
Asymmetric editorial grid:
- lead story: 7 columns;
- secondary: 5 columns;
- next row: three cards.

### Card data
- category;
- title;
- subject;
- excerpt;
- issue;
- image.

---

## 06. Developers' Diary

### Purpose
Give the recurring real-estate / built-environment series its own signature presence.

### Layout
Split section:
- large architectural / portrait visual;
- editorial copy + 2 profile links.

### Accent
Gold + issue color detail.
Do not make it look like a property-sales website.

---

## 07. Why LEGEND

### Purpose
Communicate editorial philosophy.

### Layout
Large serif quote-like heading.
Narrow body column.
Optional oversized issue/page number motif.

### Background
Dark ink or paper, whichever gives best pacing after Developers' Diary.

---

## 08. Editorial Verticals

### Layout
Large text list rather than small icon cards.

Desktop:
Hovering a vertical can update the image panel.

Mobile:
Stacked list or horizontal chip list + static image.

No unnecessary icons.

---

## 09. Pitch Your Story

### Purpose
Convert potential editorial subjects.

### Action
CTA opens/scrolls to Google Form embed or opens Google Form in new tab.

### Required disclaimer
Submission does not guarantee publication.

---

## 10. Partnerships

### Purpose
Commercial enquiries.

### Action
Separate Google Form.

### Visual distinction
Keep editorial and commercial flows separate.

---

## 11. Footer

### Include
- logo;
- tagline;
- navigation;
- contact email once client confirms;
- social links;
- Mumbai location;
- copyright.

### Optional
Small “Latest issue” cover thumbnail.
